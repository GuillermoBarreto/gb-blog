Hey. I'm Guillermo.

I'm studying software engineering at WGU — nights and weekends, around work. I've been writing code every single day, pushing small commits to my repos each night. Weekends I play violin at church.

I started this blog for a selfish reason: I learn by writing things down. If I can't explain something simply, I don't understand it yet. So this is me figuring things out in public.

What you'll get here: tech news translated into plain English — what it actually means for people learning this stuff — and notes on the craft. Debugging. Fundamentals. The boring parts that turn out to matter most.

I'm not an expert. I'm a student with a laptop and a lot of questions. If that's you too, stick around.

New posts regularly. Thanks for reading — really.
