Welcome to my blog. This is a small corner of the internet where I'll be thinking out loud about technology.

## Who I am

I'm Guillermo Barreto, a software engineering student at Western Governors University. I'm early in my journey as a developer — learning the fundamentals, building projects, and figuring out how this whole industry works from the inside.

## What I'll write about

Two things, mostly:

1. **Tech news** — what's happening in the industry, and what those stories actually mean for people learning to build software.
2. **The craft** — the fundamentals of writing code, debugging, and learning effectively. The unglamorous stuff that actually matters.

## Why a blog?

Writing forces clarity. Every post here is me trying to understand something well enough to explain it — and if it helps someone else along the way, even better.

New posts every week. Thanks for reading.
