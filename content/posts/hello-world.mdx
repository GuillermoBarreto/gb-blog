Hey. I'm Guillermo.

I'm a software engineering student at Western Governors University, and I started this blog for a simple reason: I'm learning in public now. For a long time I kept my head down — tutorials, coursework, figuring things out alone. That works, but it's lonely. And honestly? Writing things down is how I actually learn them.

## A little about me

By day I work, by night I study. Somewhere in between I ship code — I've got a streak going of pushing commits every single night, small improvements across my repos. Weekends you'll find me playing violin with the worship team at my church.

Right now I'm in that in-between phase: not a beginner anymore, not a professional yet. I'm applying to IT roles, grinding through interview prep, and trying to close the gap between where I am and where I want to be. If you're in a similar spot, this blog is for you too.

## What you'll find here

Two things, mostly:

1. **Tech news, translated.** Not the headlines — what the headlines *mean* for people like us who are still learning. When Google launches chips into space, I want to know why that matters for someone studying data structures at midnight.
2. **The craft.** The unglamorous stuff: debugging, fundamentals, learning how to learn. The things tutorials skip.

## Why "hello, world"?

Every programmer starts there. It's the smallest possible program that proves the setup works. This blog is my setup. Let's see what it can do.

New posts regularly. Thanks for stopping by — seriously.
